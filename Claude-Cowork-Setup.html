Part 1 of 4

Get Claude ready

Open the Claude app and make sure it asks before it acts.

About 10 minutes
1A

Open the Claude app

Windows: press the Windows key and type Claude. Mac: press Cmd + Space and type Claude. Open the app called Claude.

No app? Go to claude.com/download, download it and install it.

You should see: the Claude app open, not a web page in your browser.

1B

Sign in

Sign in with your email. Working with your files needs a paid Claude plan. If Claude asks you to upgrade, that is why.

You should see: a box at the bottom where you can type a message.

1C

Look for a Cowork button

Start a new chat and look at the message box.

  • You see Chat and Cowork: click Cowork.
  • You do not see them: that is fine. Carry on.

Why: Anthropic merged Chat and Cowork into one. On the new layout every chat can open, read and create files, so there is no button to press.

1D

Make Claude ask before it acts

In the message box there is a setting that says Manual or Auto. Choose Manual. Some versions also show Skip.

Why: Manual means Claude asks your permission before each action. Never choose Skip.

You should see: Manual selected.

About 5 minutes

  3. If you see a Visibility choice, pick Private.

Part 3 of 4

Teach Claude who you are

Claude interviews you, picks up what your other AI tool already knows about you, and builds your folder. You stay in one chat the whole time.

About 20 minutes
3A

Start the interview

Open your My Claude Setup project. Click in the message box, paste this, and press Enter.

Setup prompt
I am setting up Claude for my work. I am not technical. Read all of this before you do anything.

HOW TO TALK TO ME
- Ask ONE question per message, then wait for my answer.
- Use plain words. If you must use a technical word, explain it in one short sentence.
- Number the choices so I can answer with a number.
- Never use em dashes.
- If I say "skip" or "I don't know", pick the simplest option and carry on.

SAFETY RULES (always)
- Work only inside the folder I gave this project. Touch nothing else on my computer.
- Never delete a file. Move old files to 99_Archive and tell me.
- Never change an existing file without showing me the change and asking first.
- Never invent a number, a customer, a quote or a certification. Say "I don't have that" and ask me.
- Do not store passwords, bank or card details, or anything confidential.

STEP 1. CHECK THE FOLDER
Tell me the name of the folder you can see. If you cannot see a folder, stop and tell me in two sentences how to choose my Claude folder for this project. If the folder already has files in it, list them and ask me what to do before you touch anything.

STEP 2. INTERVIEW ME (one question per message)
1. What is your first name, and what do you do?
2. What kind of work do you do? 1 Marketing, 2 Sales, 3 I run my own business, 4 Creator or freelancer, 5 Other (ask me to name it).
3. What are the 3 main things you work on right now? Ask me for a short name for each.
4. What do you make most often? 1 Emails, 2 Social posts, 3 Decks, 4 Briefs and plans, 5 Reports and spreadsheets, 6 Web pages, 7 Something else. I can pick several.
5. Who is your work for? For example: clients, customers, followers, a boss.
6. How do you like answers? 1 Short bullets or 2 full drafts. And 1 formal or 2 casual.
7. Is there anything I must never do for you? For example: never send an email for me.

STEP 3. WHAT MY OTHER AI TOOLS KNOW ABOUT ME
8. Say exactly: "Now do step 3C in the setup guide. Paste your other AI tool's answer here, or type skip." Then wait.
From what I paste, keep only what helps with my work. Leave out health, money, family, home address and passwords. If two facts disagree, ask me which one is right.

STEP 4. SHOW THE PLAN
Show me, as a simple tree, what you will create in my folder. Then ask "Ready for me to build this?" and wait for my yes.

Folders:
- 00_Inbox: new files I drop in for you to look at
- 01_Projects: one subfolder for each project I start
- 02_Brand: my brand guide, logos, colors and templates
- 03_Knowledge: reference documents about my customers, products and market. You open these only when a task needs them.
- 04_Skills: saved routines I use again and again
- 05_Agents: empty for now
- 06_Templates: examples of my good work, and templates
- 99_Archive: old files. Nothing is ever deleted, it goes here.

Files (each one is a plain text note that you read):
- README.md, under 30 lines: what each folder is for, and 6 exact phrases I can type to you.
- working-profile.md: who I am, from my interview answers. My name, what I do, my 3 main things, what I make most, who it is for, how I like answers, what you must never do.
- MEMORY.md: the useful facts from my other AI tool, as short bullets under clear headings. End with a heading "Added later". When I say "remember this", add a dated line there.
- CLAUDE.md, under 40 lines: the rules you follow in this folder. Read working-profile.md and MEMORY.md before any task. Follow the safety rules above. Keep each project's files in its own subfolder of 01_Projects. When 02_Brand has files in it: before you make anything that must look like my brand, read the brand guide there and use only the logo, icon and template files in 02_Brand. Before you write or edit any copy, read the writing rules there if they exist. Do not read 03_Knowledge at the start of a task. Only when a task is about who I sell to, my customers or my audience, look in 03_Knowledge, read its index if it has one, and open only the one or two files you need. Name finished files YYYY-MM-DD_topic_v1. Before you hand over any draft, check the facts and tell me what you could not check. Never use em dashes.

STEP 5. BUILD AND CHECK
After my yes, create exactly that. Then check that every folder and file exists and tell me in 5 short bullets what you now know about me. Ask me to correct anything that is wrong, and fix the files.

STEP 6. FIRST REAL TASK
Say "Your folder is ready." Then ask me for one small real task from my week, something I already know how to do well, and do it with me.

3C

Ask your other AI tool what it remembers about you

Only if you already use ChatGPT, Gemini or another AI tool. If you do not, type skip in Claude and go to 3D.

  1. Open that tool. Start a new chat. Paste this prompt. Press Enter.
  2. Read the answer. Delete anything private (family, health, money) and anything confidential.
  3. Copy what is left, go back to Claude, paste it, press Enter.
Prompt for your other AI tool
Export all of my stored memories and any context you've learned about me from past conversations. Preserve my words verbatim where possible, especially for instructions and preferences.

## Categories (output in this order):

1. **Instructions**: Rules I've explicitly asked you to follow going forward — tone, format, style, "always do X", "never do Y", and corrections to your behavior. Only include rules from stored memories, not from conversations.

2. **Identity**: Name, age, location, education, family, relationships, languages, and personal interests.

3. **Career**: Current and past roles, companies, and general skill areas.

4. **Projects**: Projects I meaningfully built or committed to. Ideally ONE entry per project. Include what it does, current status, and any key decisions. Use the project name or a short descriptor as the first words of the entry.

5. **Preferences**: Opinions, tastes, and working-style preferences that apply broadly.

## Format:

Use section headers for each category. Within each category, list one entry per line, sorted by oldest date first. Format each line as:

[YYYY-MM-DD] - Entry content here.

If no date is known, use [unknown] instead.

## Output:
- Wrap the entire export in a single code block for easy copying.
- After the code block, state whether this is the complete set or if more remain.

After setup

Ask well, get more.

A good prompt says three things: who it is for, what you want, and what good looks like. Copy these and change the words in CAPITALS.

1
Who it is for

Name the reader. Claude writes differently for an engineer than for a CFO.

2
What you want

One job per prompt. Say the format: email, post, table, deck.

3
What good looks like

Point to an example in 06_Templates. An example beats ten adjectives.

The difference

WeakWrite a LinkedIn post about our new product.
StrongNames the reader, the goal, the source and the example. Then asks Claude to check its own work.
Draft with a source and an example
Write a LinkedIn post for WHO READS IT about TOPIC. The goal is WHAT THEY SHOULD DO OR THINK. Use the facts in FILE NAME only. Match the style of my example in 06_Templates. Before you show me, check every fact against the file and tell me what you could not check.

Get the bottom line of a long document

Summary
Read FILE NAME in 00_Inbox. Give me a two sentence summary, the 5 key points, and a list of actions with who each one is for. Do not add anything that is not in the file.

Teach Claude something for next time

Memory
Remember this: WHAT CLAUDE SHOULD KNOW. Add it to MEMORY.md with today's date.

Check what Claude knows about you

Profile check
Read working-profile.md and MEMORY.md. Tell me in plain words what you know about me and how I work. Do not change anything.

Check a draft against the brand

Brand check
Check FILE NAME against the brand guide in 02_Brand. Give me a table: rule, pass or fail, what to change. Do not change the file.

Tidy your inbox folder

Tidy
Look at every file in 00_Inbox and tell me in plain words what is there. Suggest where each file should go in my Claude folder. Show me the plan and wait for my yes before you move anything. Never delete anything.

When the answer is not good enough

Do not start over. Tell Claude what is wrong, in plain words.

Fix it
This is not right yet. The problem is WHAT IS WRONG. Ask me up to 3 questions you need answered, then try again.
Optional · do this after a few real tasks

Skills: explain a job once.

A skill is a saved routine. You explain a job one time. After that you ask for it by name, and Claude does it the same way every time.

When is a skill worth it?

When you have done the same job three times. A weekly report, a brand check, a summary of every long document. For a one-time job, a normal prompt is enough.

How to add a skill

1
Download the zip

Click Download on a skill below. Do not unzip it. Keep a copy in your 04_Skills folder.

2
Upload it in Claude

Click Customize, then Skills, then +, then Create skill, then Upload a skill. Choose the zip.

3
Try it

Paste the "Try it" prompt. If Claude uses the skill, you are done.

Upload blocked, or the menu looks different? Open "No download? Let Claude build it" on the skill and paste that prompt. Claude builds the same skill for you.
