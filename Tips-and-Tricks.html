Small moves that save you hours.

16 tips. Fourteen work today, and two need a small setup first. Each one starts with a real problem, then gives you the steps and a prompt to copy. Click a tip to open it.

Save your limit

Turn long files into Markdown before Claude reads them

A 60 page PDF can eat a big piece of your limit in one go.
Works today

What you get: The same words, for far fewer tokens. A PDF also sends a picture of every page. A Markdown file sends only the words.

  1. Open a free AI tool you already have, such as Copilot, ChatGPT or Gemini, and attach the PDF or paste the long email.
  2. Paste the prompt below.
  3. Copy the answer into Notepad and save it in 00_Inbox with a name that ends in .md
  4. Tell Claude: Read the .md file in 00_Inbox.
Paste this in the other AI tool
Turn this document into clean Markdown. Keep every heading, list and number. Leave out images, page numbers and footers. Give me only the Markdown, in one block.

Good to know: Use this when you only need the words. Keep the original file when the charts, tables or layout matter.

New task, new chat

Claude rereads the whole chat with every message. A chat that runs all day gets expensive.
Works today

What you get: A fresh, light start, without losing what you decided.

  1. When a chat gets long, paste the prompt below.
  2. Copy the summary.
  3. Start a new chat and paste it as your first message.
Paste this in the long chat
Sum up what we decided in this chat in 8 short bullets, so I can paste it into a new chat and continue there.

Let Gemini or Copilot find it, let Claude write it

Claude cannot see your inbox or your calendar. Gemini (for Google) or Copilot (for Microsoft) can.
Works today

What you get: Facts from your emails and meetings, turned into a finished deck or brief.

  1. Ask Gemini or Copilot for the facts. For example: what a client asked for last week.
  2. Read its answer and delete anything confidential.
  3. Paste it into Claude with the prompt below.
Paste this in Claude, with the answer under it
Here are my notes from a meeting. Turn them into WHAT YOU NEED, for WHO READS IT. Use only what is in the notes. Tell me what is missing.

Get better answers

Let Claude interview you first

You do not know what to put in the prompt. So you write four words.
Works today

What you get: Claude asks for what it needs. You only answer.

  1. Paste the prompt below and fill in your task.
  2. Answer the questions, one at a time.
Paste this in Claude
I want help with YOUR TASK. Before you write anything, ask me the questions you need answered to do it well. One question at a time.

Talk instead of typing

Good prompts are longer than we like to type.
Works today

What you get: A full, natural brief in 30 seconds. People say much more than they type.

  1. Click in the Claude message box.
  2. Press the Windows key + H. A small microphone appears. On a Mac, switch on Dictation under System Settings, Keyboard.
  3. Talk as if you are briefing a colleague. Then press Enter.

Good to know: Voice typing needs an internet connection and a microphone. Do not dictate anything private with other people around.

Show an example of good work

You describe the style you want with ten adjectives. Claude still misses it.
Works today

What you get: Claude copies the structure, the length and the tone of a real piece.

  1. Put one or two pieces of work you are proud of in 06_Templates
  2. Add the line below to your prompt.
Add this line to any prompt
Match the style, length and structure of my example in 06_Templates. Do not copy its content.

Good to know: Anthropic calls examples one of the most reliable ways to steer the result.

Make Claude check its own work

You spend as long checking the draft as you would have spent writing it.
Works today

What you get: A draft that comes with its own list of what is checked and what is not.

  1. Add the lines below to the end of your prompt.
Add this to the end of a prompt
Before you show me the result: check every number, name and date against the source file. If something is not in the file, write "not in the file" and do not guess. End with one line: Checked / Could not check.

Ask for the plan first

Claude ran for five minutes and built the wrong thing.
Works today

What you get: You catch a wrong direction in ten seconds, before any work is done.

  1. Add the line below to any larger job.
  2. Read the plan. Say yes, or say what to change.

Say what is wrong, do not start over

The answer is 80 percent right. You delete the chat and try again from zero.
Works today

What you get: A fix in one message, with everything Claude already knows kept.

  1. Tell Claude what is wrong, in plain words.
  2. Or paste the prompt below.
Paste this in the same chat
This is not right yet. The problem is WHAT IS WRONG. Ask me up to 3 questions you need answered, then try again.

Teach Claude once

You correct the same thing every week.
Works today

What you get: Claude writes it into your MEMORY.md file and uses it from then on.

  1. When you correct Claude, add the line below.
Paste this after your correction
Remember this: WHAT CLAUDE SHOULD KNOW. Add it to MEMORY.md with today's date.

Good to know: You can open MEMORY.md in Notepad at any time to read or fix what Claude remembers.

Video and audio

Get the key points of a YouTube video

A 40 minute webinar. You need the five things that matter.
Works today

What you get: The key points in a minute, and the exact words when you need a quote.

  1. Open the video in Chrome.
  2. For the key points: click Ask Gemini at the top of Chrome and ask for them.
  3. For the exact words: open the video description and click Show transcript. Copy the text into a file in 00_Inbox
  4. Give that file to Claude for a summary, a post or a brief.
Ask Gemini this
Give me the 5 key points of this video, each with the time it is said.

Good to know: Show transcript only appears on videos that have captions. If you do not see the Ask Gemini button, your country or version does not have it yet.

Get a transcript of a recording that is not on YouTube

A recorded call, a webinar file, a customer interview. Nobody will watch an hour of it.
Works today

What you get: The full text, ready for Claude to turn into notes, posts or a brief.

  1. A recorded video call: Teams, Zoom and Google Meet can save a transcript with the recording. Download it.
  2. Any other recording: open Word in your browser. Click Home, then Dictate, then Transcribe, then Upload audio.
  3. Save the text in 00_Inbox
  4. Give it to Claude with the prompt below.
Paste this in Claude
Read the transcript in 00_Inbox. Give me a two sentence summary, the 5 key points, every decision, and a list of actions with who each one is for. Use only what is in the transcript.

Good to know: Word accepts .wav, .mp4, .m4a and .mp3 files, up to 300 minutes a month, with a Microsoft 365 subscription. Ask the people in the recording before you transcribe it.

Let Claude cut and subtitle a video

You need a 30 second clip with subtitles from a long recording. The video team is busy.
Needs setup

What you get: A trimmed clip, a vertical version, or burned-in subtitles, made from a plain request.

  1. Install FFmpeg (free). For subtitles made on your own computer, also Whisper (free).
  2. Put the video in a new folder: 01_Projects / video
  3. Paste the prompt below and fill in what you want.
  4. Read the plan. Say yes. Claude makes a new file and never changes the original.
Paste this in Claude
There is a video in 01_Projects/video. First check that FFmpeg is installed. If it is not, stop and tell me.

What I want: DESCRIBE THE EDIT. For example: cut from 02:10 to 02:45, make it vertical, add subtitles.

Rules: show me your plan first and wait for my yes. Never change the original file. Save the result as a new file in the same folder. Tell me what you could not do.

Good to know: Free tools, from their official pages: FFmpeg at ffmpeg.org. Whisper at github.com/openai/whisper. yt-dlp at github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp, only for videos you have the right to download.

In the browser

Let Claude do the clicking

Downloading 20 assets from a portal. Filling in the same web form again and again.
Needs setup

What you get: Claude in Chrome opens the pages, clicks, types and downloads for you, while you watch.

  1. Add the Claude extension from the Chrome Web Store and sign in.
  2. Open the website in Chrome and click the Claude icon.
  3. Describe the job with the prompt below.
  4. Keep it on Manual so it asks before each action.
Paste this in Claude in Chrome
On this website, WHAT YOU WANT DONE. For example: download every logo file on this page into my Downloads folder. Ask me before you submit any form, send anything or log in anywhere.

Good to know: Anthropic advises against using it on pages with sensitive information. Close tabs with banking or other private data first. You stay responsible for what it does in your name.

Ask about the page you are looking at

A long article, a competitor's product page, a dense report in your browser.
Works today

What you get: A summary or an answer about that page, without copying anything.

  1. Click Ask Gemini at the top of Chrome.
  2. Ask your question about the page.
Ask Gemini this
Sum up this page in 5 bullets. Then tell me what it says about TOPIC.

Good to know: If you do not see the button, your country or version does not have it yet.

Show Claude your screen

An error message, a chart, a strange setting. Hard to describe in words.
Works today

What you get: Claude sees what you see and explains it.

  1. Press Windows key + Shift + S (Mac: Cmd + Ctrl + Shift + 4) and drag over the part of the screen.
  2. Click in the Claude message box and press Ctrl + V (Mac: Cmd + V).
  3. Ask your question.

Good to know: A picture uses more tokens than text. Crop it tight, and leave out anything confidential.
